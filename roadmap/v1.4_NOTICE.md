# Notice of Opening — SOX v1.4

SOX v1.31.1 establishes absolute insolvency at 𝓘 = 1.

Empirical systems exist that appear to survive at this boundary.

This contradiction does not falsify SOX.
It motivates a new rule.

SOX v1.4 will formalize **Capitulation Local**:
the controlled localization of insolvency to preserve global solvency.
